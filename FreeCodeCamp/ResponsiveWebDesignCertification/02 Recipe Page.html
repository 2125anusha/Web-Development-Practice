Fudgy Chocolate Chip Brownies Recipe

Low calorie gluten free recipe

Ingredients

  • ½ cup coconut oil (melted and slightly cooled)
  • ½ cup honey or maple syrup
  • 2 large eggs (at room temperature)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ⅓ cup chocolate chips (for sprinkling on top before baking)
  • 1 cup fresh raspberries (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Preheat and Prep: Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C). Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper or grease it lightly with a little coconut oil.
  2. Mix the Wet Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the melted coconut oil and honey (or maple syrup). Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  3. Add the Dry Ingredients: Sift in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt. Stir gently with a spatula just until the flour disappears. Do not overmix, or the brownies will lose their fudgy texture.
  4. Spread and Sprinkle: Pour the batter into your prepared pan and smooth the top. Sprinkle the chocolate chips evenly over the entire surface.
  5. Bake: Bake for 20 to 22 minutes. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out with a few moist crumbs attached, but no wet batter.
  6. Cool and Serve: Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before slicing them into squares. Serve each brownie square with a generous handful of fresh raspberries on the side or crushed on top.
